Reminders Off

A notification pops up before each class while the app is open or running in the background. On a phone the system pauses apps you aren't using, so also add your timetable to your phone's calendar (below) for alerts when the app is closed.

Semester

Timetables list teaching weeks by number, like 2-9, 11-18. Set the Monday of week 1 so those numbers line up with real dates.

Add to your calendar

One event for each class in each teaching week, using the saved semester dates. The .ics file carries a reminder at your chosen time; open it on your phone or import it into Outlook or Apple Calendar. For Google Calendar, import the CSV at calendar.google.com under Settings, then Import & export.
